How to book

We host stays of 30 nights or more. Book direct and you skip the platform fees; returning guests get first call on dates. We can also be found on Airbnb if you'd rather book there.

Direct rate: $4,800 a month for either home, cleaning included, about 7% under the Airbnb price. Pay by bank transfer or cash and take $100 off each month.
