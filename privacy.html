Privacy

De Longpre Enterprises LLC ("we") operates hollywoodhideaways.com.

What we collect. When you inquire or book, we collect your name, contact details, stay dates, guest count, and the information needed to process payment. Card payments are handled by Stripe; we never see or store your full card number.

How we use it. To answer inquiries, confirm bookings, send check-in details, and comply with tax and legal obligations. We do not sell or share your information with anyone except the payment processor and as required by law.

Cookies. This site sets no tracking cookies. Stripe's checkout may set cookies needed to process payment.

Retention. Booking records are kept as long as required for tax purposes, then deleted.

Contact. stay@hollywoodhideaways.com with any question or to request deletion of your data.
